04 — Questions

Sensible answers.

How is this priced?

Two options: a one-off purchase with a yearly support fee, or a monthly licence that covers everything. Both work out a fraction of what a comparable SAP setup would cost. Get in touch and we'll have a chat about what fits your business.

How long does it take to get up and running?

Most businesses are live inside a few weeks. That includes importing your existing stock and customer data, setting up users and roles, training your staff and your drivers, and making sure everything actually works in the field before we call it done.

Can it be customised for our business?

Yes — that's the whole point. The core ERP is the same, but I'll branded it, adjust workflows to match how your business runs, and build any extras you specifically need. You're not stuck with someone else's idea of how things should work.

Where does my data live?

Hosted on UK servers by default, with daily backups. If you need it self-hosted on your own infrastructure, that's also an option.

What if something breaks?

You ring me. Or you email me. You're not going through three support tiers in another country. Support is part of the service, and it comes from the person who built the system.

Can I see a demo?

Absolutely — that's the best way to see if it's right for your business. Request a demo here and I'll be in touch to book in a call.
