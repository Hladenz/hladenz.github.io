04 / Why NextERP

Not a theme.
A system.

Most small-business ERP is either a generic platform dressed up with an appliance plugin, or enterprise software that needs a dedicated IT team to run it. NextERP is neither.

Purpose-built, not adapted

Every feature exists because an appliance business needed it. Stock is counted in individual units. Deliveries are routed by weight and volume. Service jobs track specific serial numbers. Nothing is retrofitted from another industry.

Self-managed by design

Admin tasks that other systems need a developer for — adding products, editing document templates, adjusting pricing rules, managing staff permissions — are all done through a clean browser interface. No technical knowledge required, ever.

Solid and fully auditable

Every transaction is logged. Nothing is deleted — records are voided or cancelled and kept forever. If something goes wrong, you can trace exactly what happened and who did it, down to the minute.
