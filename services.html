07 / Questions

Sensible answers.

Do all these prices include VAT?

No VAT to add. Skiddaw Digital isn't VAT-registered, so the prices you see are the prices you pay

How long does a website typically take?

Starter sites are usually two to three weeks from start to live. Standard four to six weeks. Premium and bespoke builds depend on scope we'll agree a timeline up front and stick to it.

Do I have to take a care plan?

Not at all, but once your first year of free hosting is up, your site needs a home. A care plan keeps the hosting, backups and updates running. The alternative is finding a builder who'll charge you £200 the first time something breaks at the worst possible moment.

I just need a question answered, not a project, can I still get in touch?

Yes, please do. First hour of any conversation is free, whether it ends in a project or not. If I can answer something off the bat, I will.

What if my project doesn't fit any of these?

Drop me a line. Custom software in particular is rarely identical from one job to the next these prices are starting points and we'd sort a proper quote after a conversation.

Can you work with businesses outside West Cumbria?

West Cumbria and the Lake District come first that's the whole point of Skiddaw Digital. I'll travel to anywhere in the Lakes for an in-person meet, and beyond that I'm happy to work remotely if it's the right fit.
