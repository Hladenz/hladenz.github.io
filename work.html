/ 01 — Hospitality · Website

Hawcliffe House — a guesthouse, online.

A family-run B&B in Keswick needed a website that looked as warm as the place itself, and that guests could actually use to book a room without faff.

Hawcliffe House is the kind of B&B people come back to year after year. The brief was simple: build a site that feels like the place — warm, welcoming, well-kept — and that makes it easy for new guests to find them, see the rooms, and get in touch.

I built a clean, fast site that puts the rooms, the location, and the contact details where they should be — front and centre. No bloat, no pop-ups, no cookie-banner war. Just a website that works on a phone in the carpark of the Lake District.

/ 02 — Retail & logistics · Software

Genesis Home Appliances — running the operation.

A growing white goods business needed software that could actually keep up with sales, deliveries and stock without falling back on spreadsheets and phone calls.

Genesis Home Appliances came to me with the usual problem: the business had outgrown its tools. Sales were going in one place, stock in another, drivers were getting routes by WhatsApp, and end-of-month reporting was a job no one wanted.

I built a full custom ERP around how the business actually works — order through to doorstep. Sales staff log orders in one place, stock updates in real time, routes are planned automatically and pushed to a driver app on the van, and management get the reports they need without anyone wrangling spreadsheets at midnight.

That same platform is now available to other white-goods and appliance businesses as a productised system — have a look at the ERP system →
