/ 02 Retail · Pricing, sales site & POS

Genesis Home Appliances —
priced sharp, sold fast.

A growing white-goods retailer needed to price competitively without trawling rivals by hand, and to sell faster on the shop floor with a system built around how their staff actually work.

Client
Genesis Home Appliances
Scope
Pricing engine · in-store sales site · POS device
Built with
Python · web scraping · custom POS hardware integration
Outcome
Sharper pricing, faster sales floor

Genesis is a growing white-goods retailer running serious volume. Two problems were eating into the business: pricing competitively across hundreds of SKUs meant somebody manually checking competitor sites every day, and the shop-floor sales process was slow because the systems they had didn't really suit how the team worked.

I built a pricing engine in Python that scrapes competitor listings on a schedule, compares them against Genesis's own catalogue, and surfaces the products that need a price change to stay competitive. What used to take half a person's morning now runs by itself overnight.

On top of that I built a custom internal sales website for staff to use in the showroom, paired with a POS device for easy sales floor usage. Same brand, same data, same prices coming straight from the pricing engine. Staff can quote, sell and take payment in one flow, with none of the off-the-shelf clutter.

The result was a great business outcome the kind you can point at on a P&L. Sharper pricing where it mattered, faster sales on the floor, and the team freed up to focus on the parts of the business that matter most.

A complete ERP system I built independently of any client work, purpose-designed for white-goods and appliance businesses. Five modules sales, stock, route planning, a driver app and reporting all talking to each other so your business doesn't have to wrangle spreadsheets.

The full feature set is comparable to SAP, but priced and shaped for small and medium businesses rather than enterprises. Sales staff log orders, stock updates live, routes auto-build by postcode and load, and the driver app on the van syncs proof of delivery back to the office in real time.
